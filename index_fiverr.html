About

Curiosity led me to design. Problem-solving made me stay.

My journey into product design began with a curiosity about computers and how digital products work. While developing my computer skills, I discovered UI/UX design and became interested in how technology could be made clearer and easier for people to use.

I began learning independently through Coursera and YouTube before investing in specialised courses, practical training and internships. Over time, I progressed from learning Figma to designing end-to-end experiences across mobile applications, web platforms, marketplaces, AI products and service-based platforms.

My background in Microbiology strengthened my curiosity, analytical thinking and interest in research. Today, I apply those qualities to understanding users, structuring complex information and creating thoughtful digital experiences.
