Interview Design Challenge

Making crypto-tax services feel clearer and more approachable

Taxora is a crypto-tax preparation and advisory concept designed to help cryptocurrency investors understand tax obligations, consolidate complex financial information and move towards filing with greater confidence.

Role

Sole UI/UX Designer

Timeline

1 day

Platform

Desktop website

Type

Design challenge

Problem

Crypto-tax services can feel technical, fragmented and intimidating. The website needed to communicate expertise and trust while making a complex service easier to understand.

Constraint

The complete design was created within one day.

Process

Because of the short timeline, I moved directly from content hierarchy and visual exploration into high-fidelity design rather than producing formal low-fidelity wireframes.

Design priorities

  • •Establish trust early
  • •Explain the service clearly
  • •Create a logical conversion journey
  • •Balance financial credibility with a modern crypto identity
  • •Use interface previews to make the service feel tangible
  • •Maintain strong hierarchy across a long landing page

Final screens

Desktop landing page · full length View full page →

Outcome

A complete conversion-focused desktop landing-page concept delivered within the one-day assessment timeline. It was not launched.

Reflection

Future development could include mobile adaptation, accessibility review, usability testing, and validation of the content hierarchy with target users, none of which have happened yet.
