Kokopay & Kokosend · Fintech · Paid client work

Two fintech websites built on one shared system

Kokopay is an app for trading crypto, sending money and paying bills across Africa. Kokosend moves money between Nigeria, Kenya, Ghana, South Africa and Tanzania. I redesigned both websites as the only designer, with about five days per site, in light and dark mode.
