About

I learned to build before I learned to design.

I started in 2020 as a freelance front-end developer, building websites in HTML, CSS and JavaScript while studying Microbiology at Obafemi Awolowo University. Turning other people's designs into working pages made me curious about the decisions behind them.

By 2022 I was designing products myself, starting with a mobile app, website and admin dashboard for one client. Since then I have designed salon software, fintech and lending websites, and e-commerce, and I still build: Fancrest and Togedab are sites I designed and shipped myself.

Knowing how things get built changes how I design. I hand over screens developers can actually implement, and my science background keeps me asking for evidence before I decide.
